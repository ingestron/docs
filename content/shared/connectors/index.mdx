A connection's `connector` says what kind of source it is. The packs in your
standard decide how it is read; some sources can be read in more than one
way, depending on how they can be reached
([reachability](/docs/concepts/choose-a-platform#reachability)).

To compare platform combinations first, read
[Compare the platforms](/docs/concepts/choose-a-platform). Each connector's
page shows its routes, how to connect and read with each pack, and complete
examples. To create a project, follow
[Create a project](/docs/~/create-a-project#start-from-a-connection).
