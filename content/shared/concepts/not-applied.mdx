Your definitions say what each table should have: a check and its action, a
cleansing operation, a quarantine, a way of keeping history, who may read it.
The engine that
keeps the table does the work, in its own SQL. When that engine cannot do one
of these things, Ingestron says so at the line that asks for it, with the
engine's reason. That state is **not applied here**, the warning
[`NOT_APPLIED`](/docs/reference/errors#NOT_APPLIED).

It is always a warning. The files are still written and run; the table goes
without that feature on that engine. The same definitions can be applied
in full on one engine and not on another.

## What it covers [#what-it-covers]

Each table is judged against the pack that keeps it: its store step, or its
ingest step when that step writes the table or the table ends at the landed
files. Another pack in the same standard does not count. Not applied can be:

- a contract rule's kind<Only on="synapse">, such as `pattern`, since
  serverless T-SQL has no regular expressions</Only>; an action<Only on="synapse">,
  such as `fail`, since a view cannot stop a load</Only>; or a table check;
- a cleansing operation, `deduplicate`, or a time zone conversion<Only on="synapse">,
  including the
  [zones Synapse cannot convert](/docs/reference/packs/synapse#quality-and-cleansing)</Only>;
- quarantine, or the results of the checks;
- a value of [intent](/docs/reference/intent#support), such as `load.mode` on a
  method that reads the source itself;
- a contract field the pack does not apply yet;
- a role's grant, its `write`, exact grants, a mask or a row filter<Only on="duckdb">:
  any of them, since DuckDB has no access control</Only><Only on="synapse">,
  such as a mask, since a view over files cannot mask</Only>; a table one pack
  keeps and another serves is judged by both;
- cleansing a table that ends at the landed files, since nothing reads those
  files before your reports do.

What the pack applies, and why it does not, is on its page under
**Quality and cleansing**, **Access** and **Intent**:

  [the DuckDB pack](/docs/reference/packs/duckdb#quality-and-cleansing) (its

</Only>
<Only on="synapse">

## Where it shows [#where-it-shows]

**In `check`, `plan` and `apply`,** under its own heading,
`Not applied on this platform`, after the other problems and warnings,
grouped by the environments that have it. For example:

<Only on="duckdb">

This is what the [first project](/docs/duckdb/first-project) prints:

```text
Not applied on this platform
dev:
contracts/orders.yaml:11:59  warning  NOT_APPLIED
  The notNull rule isn't applied on duckdb: it is applied only in a layer whose does lists check.
  Add `check` to the layer's `does` to apply it here.
```

</Only>

<Only on="synapse">

A contract's `pattern` rule, in the
[quality example](/docs/synapse/add-quality-checks):

```text
Not applied on this platform
synapse:
contracts/readings.yaml:14:40  warning  NOT_APPLIED
  The pattern rule isn't applied on synapse: serverless T-SQL has no regular expressions.
```

</Only>

<Only on="databricks">

A value of the standard a method does not use, from
[a published standard with a connection](/docs/databricks/start-from-a-published-standard#with-a-connection):

```text
Not applied on this platform
dev, prod:
standard.yaml:28:12  warning  NOT_APPLIED
  load.mode: files isn't applied on databricks: Managed ingestion reads the source, not files.
```

</Only>

<Only on="adf">

Cleansing asked of a table that ends at the landed files:

```text
Not applied on this platform
dev, prod:
flows/orders.yaml:13:27  warning  NOT_APPLIED
  Cleansing "case" of "customer" isn't applied on adf: the table ends at the landed files, and nothing cleanses them.
  Add a store step to have it cleansed.
```

</Only>

Each message says what is not applied, on which pack, and why. A warning never
changes the exit code, so `check` in CI still passes.

**In VS Code,** as a warning at the same line, with the same message. A
warning the platform [accepts](/docs/~/accept-a-warning) is not shown.

**In a data product's passport,** in its Quality section: each check the
engine does not apply is listed with its rule in words, then `Not enforced:`
and the reason, and each cleansing operation it does not apply says
`is not applied:` and the reason. Its Who may use it section says
`Not enforced on <engine>:` and the reason for each role, mask or filter the
engine does not apply; see
[Read the passport](/docs/~/define-a-data-product#read-the-passport).
The passport says this whether or not the warning is accepted: accepting a
warning quiets it, and does not make the check run or the role granted.

## A layer that does not check [#layers]

The most common case is a table in a layer that does not say it checks: your
engine runs checks only in a layer whose `does` lists `check`, or is `raw`,
where checks only count. The hint
says what to add. Write each layer as an object with what it does:

```yaml
layers:
  - { name: bronze, does: raw } # every row as it arrived; checks only count
  - { name: silver, does: [check] }
```

With these layers the first project's plan lists no warning, and `run.sql`
counts the orders that have no `order_id`. See
[Say which layers check](/docs/~/add-quality-checks#layers) and
[What a layer does](/docs/concepts/layers#what-a-layer-does).

</Only>

## How it differs from other warnings [#other-warnings]

- **Most warnings** say something in your definitions could be better: a
  setting has moved ([`SETTING_MOVED`](/docs/reference/errors#SETTING_MOVED)),
  `deduplicate` is redundant ([`DEDUPLICATE_MODE`](/docs/reference/errors#DEDUPLICATE_MODE)),
  or quarantine has nothing to keep
  ([`QUARANTINE_UNUSED`](/docs/reference/errors#QUARANTINE_UNUSED)). Not
  applied says your definitions are fine and this engine cannot do what they
  ask, so it is listed apart.
- **[`CAPABILITY_OFF`](/docs/reference/errors#CAPABILITY_OFF)** says the
  engine can do it, but the platform has not turned it on<Only on="databricks">,
  such as table checks without `databricks.manage.checks`, tags without
  `databricks.manage.tags`, or a contract's roles without
  `databricks.manage.grants`</Only><Only on="synapse">, such as a contract's
  roles without `synapse.manage.grants`</Only>. Its fix turns it on
  in `platform.yaml`, and the hint says what it needs. Not applied has no
  switch to turn.
- **[`QUALITY_RULE_NOT_CHECKED`](/docs/reference/errors#QUALITY_RULE_NOT_CHECKED)**
  says no engine could check a rule as it is written, such as a `custom` rule
  or one with no comparison.
- **[`PREVIEW_FEATURE`](/docs/reference/errors#PREVIEW_FEATURE)** says the
  feature is applied, but it is in preview on the platform, and the standard
  asks for generally available features only.

## What to do [#what-to-do]

Read the reason, then choose one:

- **Move it where it is applied.** Add the duty to the layer's `does`, as the
  hint says<Only on="databricks">; check a table that Lakeflow Connect writes
  in a transform in the next layer</Only>; give a table that ends at the
  landed files a store step.
- **Choose another method or pack** that applies it, if your platform has one.
- **Remove it,** when the table can do without it.
- **Accept it,** when you have looked at it and decided to live with it, with
  the reason in `platform.yaml`; see
  [Accept a warning](/docs/~/accept-a-warning).
