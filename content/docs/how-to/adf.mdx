The ADF pack handles copying to landing storage. Project creation offers it
with Databricks for downstream tables, with
[Synapse views](/docs/platforms/synapse) or
[DuckDB tables](/docs/platforms/duckdb) over the landed files, or
on its own to [land files only](/docs/platforms/landing-only). See
[supported combinations](/docs/reference/packs) before starting.
