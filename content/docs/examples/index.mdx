Examples are complete projects with synthetic values; generate their output with Plan and Apply.
Use them to inspect how definitions fit together. Follow
[Create a project](/docs/how-to/start-from-a-template#from-a-downloaded-example) to adapt and regenerate
one; source access and deployment are separate steps.
