Each document starts with `apiVersion: ingestron.io/v1`, its `kind` and an
`id`. The fields below come from the documents' schemas, with where to find a
value and a suggestion for each field that has them, as the editor's hover
gives them. For how the
documents work together, see [Definitions](/docs/concepts/definitions).

Your editor can check these as you type: run `ingestron schemas` and follow
its instructions; see
[Check files with the YAML extension](/docs/tools/vscode#check-files-with-the-yaml-extension).

## Project

`project.yaml`, at the project's root: its id, its environments, the values
documents read with `${values.…}`, and the files to load. It has no
`apiVersion` or `kind`.

## Platform

Each pack can have a section of its own, under its id, such as `databricks:`;
its fields are on the pack's page, as for
[the `databricks:` section](/docs/reference/packs/databricks#platform). The
Databricks fields below, `workspace`, `mode`, `run_as`, `compute`,
`permissions`, `manage` and `grants`, are read at the top of the platform as
before, with a
[`PLATFORM_FIELD_MOVED`](/docs/reference/errors#PLATFORM_FIELD_MOVED) warning,
and `notifications` with one that moves it to `alerts`.

<DocumentFields kind="Platform" />

See [Environments and platform](/docs/concepts/environments-and-platform), and
[Accept a warning](/docs/how-to/accept-a-warning) for `accept`.

## Data contract

Contracts use the
[Open Data Contract Standard](https://bitol-io.github.io/open-data-contract-standard/)
v3.1.0 unchanged. The table and column fields every pack reads come first,
then the other ODCS fields. To write one, with its types, keys, rules and tags,
see
[Describe a table with a contract](/docs/how-to/write-a-contract).
